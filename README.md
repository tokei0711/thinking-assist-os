What is Thinking Assist OS?

Thinking Assist OS is not designed to make AI smarter.

It is designed to help humans:

- Continue projects
- Resume work
- Capture lessons
- Improve decisions
- Reduce restart cost

The goal is simple:

Help humans remember where they are, what they learned, and what to do next.

---

Why?

Most projects fail because people lose context.

They stop.

They forget.

They restart from zero.

Thinking Assist OS reduces the cost of stopping and starting again.
